Welcome

This Healing Agent is here to offer kind, supportive conversation as you move through grief, memories, and tender moments. You can share what’s on your heart, ask for comforting words, or simply talk about your pet and the love you still feel.

This Healing Agent provides emotional support for entertainment purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical, therapeutic, or mental health advice. If you are in distress or need medical or mental health support, please contact a qualified professional or trusted person in your life.

When you’re ready, click the 💜 Healing Support button in the corner to begin chatting.
